How the Free Cooldown Works

One download per 60 minutes
Free users can download one supported video every 60 minutes. The cooldown timer starts after a successful download completes.
Failed downloads are free
If a download fails, it does not count against your allowance and does not start a cooldown.
Upgrade to Premium
SVD Premium removes the cooldown entirely for $1.99/year, with annual renewal.
